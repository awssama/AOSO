THE SAGA

Three Siblings. One Burning World.

AOSO follows the three surviving royals of the fallen Kingdom of Zafodil — and the ancient serpent Deth, scheming since the dawn of time.

AOSO was never chosen. He wasn't born to be a legend — just a child trying to survive one more day in a world that forgot peace. Until the world decided he was worth breaking.

Beside his siblings — Kazanov, a storm of grief and devotion, and Stella, silently waiting in chains — the saga stretches from the collapsing kingdoms of the North to the Korz: a realm built from fear itself, where the true enemy is not a monster, but the idea that nothing matters.

Across its first two seasons, AOSO fights for family — and then for its own soul. Because the deepest battles aren't against entities. They're against the question Arash keeps asking: does existence mean anything at all?

SEASON I · ONGOING ON WEBNOVEL

AMID THE ASHES

Before the hero — the loss. How the world burned, how the siblings were scattered, and why a legend was born from the ashes of everything taken.

01

THE TWO POWERS

Before history had a name, Deth — absolute evil — betrayed his eternal balance with Aikad, the light itself. Their war shattered galaxies and ended with Deth exiled to a forgotten world called Earth.

02

THE ORPHAN KINGS

When King Hiter dies, ten-year-old twins Fer & Clyde inherit Arash. A mysterious wizard grants them manhood — while secretly murdering every royal messenger, so the entire North seems to have abandoned them. Grief curdles into hatred.

03

THE FALL OF THE NORTH

Poisoned queens and stolen crowns turn the twins into weapons. Kingdom after kingdom burns — until King Zaf III detonates his own soul against Arash's army, buying his people one final breath.

04

THE BROKEN FAMILY

Angel flees the flames with the royal children. Sayya village is massacred for sheltering them; little Stella is dragged away in chains. To hide them from Deth's hunt, the royal bond is severed — each child grows up alone, believing the others are gone forever.

05

HEFASTOS DESCENDS

Deth summons Hell itself — the horned colossus Hefastos — to murder the child. Ozmak, Kudler, his apprentices, and little Eva stand in the fire… until Hades, King of the Underworld, rises for the price.

06

THE KREKELS MISSION

AOSO's first mission across the stars: a gem that saves the dying king of Krekels. Ambushed by the Royal Shield Battalion and caged on Neptune, he faces the illusion-realm of Sivaros — and when he believes Ozmak dead, his royal sigil ignites a power that erases an entire army. The storm begins.

WHERE SEASON 1 LEAVES US

Three siblings on three roads: a warrior forged by grief, a boy who doesn't yet know his own strength, and a princess waiting in chains. And above them all — Deth smiles.

SEASON II · HEART OF NIHILISM

HEART OF NIHILISM

Eight years later, the test is no longer strength — it's meaning. Hurled by Deth into the Korz, AOSO lands in a world built from fear itself… beside a companion who wants nothing from life except for it to end.

01

EIGHT YEARS LATER

AOSO builds ships by day and trains in secret. Kazanov walks back into the ruins of Sayya — and years of buried rage explode. His vow cracks the sky: he will free Stella, whatever stands in the way.

02

ENTER THE KORZ

Deth's spy seizes AOSO and flings him through a portal into the Korz — a realm of sectors, each one born from a different fear. Four seals open the way out. Nothing else does.

03

THE WHISPER SECTOR

Armored titans that never stop walking. Screams that tear thought apart. A shadow repeating your own words. And at its heart — Arash: a founder of the old world, immortal and bored of eternity, hunting anyone strong enough to kill him. He finds AOSO instead.

04

THE BLOOD SECTOR

Red skies, rivers of blood, tribes that worship monsters and eat their own dead. Here the sector doesn't test AOSO's strength — it tests his soul. By the time he faces the god Aigorthal, the question isn't whether he'll survive… but what will be left of him.

05

THE BLACK STAR

While AOSO drowns in the Korz, his black star ignites above the North. Kazanov flies alone into the Kingdom of Arash, shatters its dome, and carves his brother's name into the sky — the first blow of the reunion… witnessed by Deth himself.

06

THE HAND BEHIND THE THRONE

Fer and Clyde believe they rule. They rule nothing. On the palace roof, Deth and the Unknown speak of designs older than the North itself — plans even the kings were never told. The real war hasn't started yet.

THE WORLD

Of Ash and Beyond

THE KORZ

A dimension of sectors, each one grown around a fear. You enter by accident and leave only by conquest. Its old master vanished; its visitors rarely return.

KINGDOMS OF THE NORTH

Zafodil, Arash, Hera, Onil, Nymph — founded by the five children of Beatrice, undone by a lie told to two grieving boys.

THE ROYAL SIGIL

The bloodline of Zaf carries living marks — a star for each sibling. They blaze when one is in danger, and carry strength between hearts across oceans.

THE NORTHERN VILLAGE

Part town, part fortress, part academy. Home of ice mages, genius engineers, terrible coffee — and the safest place on the planet, because Honuga says so.

PLANET NEPTUNE

A lifeless world on the surface — with a black dungeon in its depths. This is where AOSO was caged after the Royal Shield ambush, where the illusion-kingdom of Sivaros appeared… then vanished whole, leaving one question: who can drag a dimension's memory into reality?

PLANET KREKELS

The destination of AOSO's first star-mission: a blue gem to save a dying king. A loyal kingdom that honored its heroes with three days of feast — never knowing one of its dreams was merely a shadow, watching.

THE UNDERWORLD

The hell-realm ruled by Hefastos and his brother Hades — layers of fire and magma, deathless armies, thrones of black stone. The attack on the Northern Village marched from here… and so did the rescue. Even Hell has those who keep their oaths.
