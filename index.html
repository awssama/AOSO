THE SAGA

Three siblings. A burning world. An ancient serpent waiting.

AOSO was never chosen. He wasn't born to be a legend — just a child trying to survive one more day in a world that forgot peace. Until the world decided he was worth breaking.

Beside his siblings — Kazanov, a storm of grief and devotion, and Stella, silent behind her chains — the saga runs from the collapsing kingdoms of the North to the Korz: a realm built from fear itself, where the true enemy isn't a monster but the idea that nothing matters.

Across two seasons, AOSO fights for his family — and then for his own soul. Because the saga's greatest battles aren't fought against entities. They're fought against the question Arash never tires of asking: does existence mean anything at all?

AMID THE ASHES

الموسم الأول

Before the hero, the loss. How the world burned, how the siblings scattered, and why the legend rose from the ashes of everything taken.

I

THE TWO POWERS

Deth — absolute evil — betrays his eternal balance with Aikad, the light itself. Their war shatters galaxies and ends with Deth exiled to a forgotten world called Earth.

II

THE ORPHAN KINGS

King Hiter dies and ten-year-old twins Fer & Clyde inherit Arash. A mysterious wizard grants them manhood — while secretly murdering every royal messenger, so the whole North seems to have abandoned them.

III

THE FALL OF THE NORTH

Poisoned queens and stolen crowns turn the twins into weapons. Kingdom after kingdom burns — until King Zaf III detonates his own soul against Arash's army, buying his people one final breath.

IV

THE BROKEN FAMILY

Angel smuggles the children from the flames; Sayya village is massacred for sheltering them; little Stella is dragged away in chains. To hide them from Deth's hunt, the royal bond is severed — each child grows up believing the others are gone forever.

V

HEFASTOS DESCENDS

Deth summons Hell itself — the horned colossus Hefastos — to murder the child. Ozmak, Kudler and little Eva stand in the fire… until Hades, King of the Underworld, rises for the price.

VI

THE KREKELS MISSION

His first star-mission becomes an ambush: a dungeon on Neptune, the illusion-realm of Sivaros — and when AOSO believes Ozmak dead, his royal sigil ignites a power that erases an entire army. The storm begins.

HEART OF NIHILISM

الموسم الثاني

Eight years later, the test is no longer strength — it's meaning. Hurled by Deth into the Korz, AOSO lands in a world built from fear itself… beside a companion who wants nothing from life except its end.

I

EIGHT YEARS LATER

AOSO builds ships by day, trains in secret. Kazanov walks back into the ruins of Sayya — and his buried rage explodes: he will free Stella, whatever stands in the way.

II

ENTER THE KORZ

Deth's spy seizes AOSO and flings him through a portal — a realm of sectors, each born from a different fear. Four seals open the way out. Nothing else does.

III

THE WHISPER SECTOR

Armored titans that never stop walking. Screams that tear thought apart. A shadow repeating your words back at you. At its heart — Arash: an immortal founder bored of eternity, hunting anyone strong enough to kill him. He finds AOSO instead.

IV

THE BLOOD SECTOR

Red skies, rivers of blood, tribes that worship monsters. Here the sector doesn't test strength — it tests the soul. By the god Aigorthal, the question isn't survival… it's what will be left of him.

V

THE BLACK STAR

While AOSO drowns in the Korz, the black star ignites. Kazanov flies alone into Arash's kingdom, shatters its dome, carves his brother's name across the sky — the reunion's first blow, witnessed by Deth himself.

VI

THE HAND BEHIND THE THRONE

Fer and Clyde believe they rule. They rule nothing. On the palace roof, Deth and the Unknown speak of designs older than the North itself. The real war hasn't started yet.

ATLAS

Of ash — and what lies beyond

THE KORZ

A dimension of sectors, each grown around a fear. You enter by accident and leave only by conquest. Its old master vanished; its visitors rarely return.

PLANET NEPTUNE

A lifeless surface with a black dungeon below. Here AOSO was caged; here the illusion-kingdom of Sivaros appeared whole, then vanished — leaving one question: who can drag a dimension's memory into reality?

PLANET KREKELS

Destination of the first mission: a blue gem for a dying king. A loyal kingdom that feasted its heroes three days — never knowing one of its dreams was a shadow, watching.

THE UNDERWORLD

Hell ruled by Hefastos and Hades — layers of fire, deathless armies. The attack on the Northern Village marched from here. So did the rescue.

KINGDOMS OF THE NORTH

Zafodil, Arash, Hera, Onil, Nymph — founded by Beatrice's five children, undone by a lie told to two grieving boys.

THE ROYAL SIGIL

The bloodline of Zaf carries living marks — a star for each sibling. They blaze when one is in danger and carry strength between hearts across oceans.

THE NORTHERN VILLAGE

Part town, part fortress, part academy. Home of ice mages — and the safest place on the planet, because Honuga says so.

THE WHISPER CORE a sector that whispers your voice back at you
THE ARMORED WHISPERER

Black armor that drinks the light; a red mask with hollow eyes that pierce thought. He never hurries — his first whisper: "You are alone here."

ECHO-WHISPERER

Gaunt, dead-green skin, a closed eye on his forehead that remembers instead of seeing. He barely speaks — he makes the world repeat what was said, until sound becomes a prison.

RUIN-WHISPERER

His mouth is a black pit that unleashes a scream defeating the body before any blade — he shields himself not with armor but with a wave that forbids approach itself.

THE SILENT WHISPERER

Half his mask is sealed shut. He erases every sound in the world — the forest's echo, your breath, your heartbeat, your very thoughts — then strikes inside that absolute silence.

THE BLOOD CORE a living heart beating beneath a red sky
THALGHOR

A faceless body of hardened blood — one deep-red eye that never blinks, watching with dark calm. Hurls daggers from nowhere and turns humans into the mutants who serve him.

FALKRETH

A walking sin: horns like roots of a dead tree, eyes that swallow light. In his chest, a bone cage — inside it a small horned, black-winged entity cradling the heart, the way a child holds its mother.

THE CHAINED SMILE

Woven from condensed shadow, skin stretched over bone about to tear. His eyes are two black voids; his mouth a permanent impossible smile — dark chains run from his skin into the darkness, binding him to something unseen.

THE ROTTEN THREE-EYED

Starved past recognition, cracked like dead earth, three warped eyes glowing a wrong red — seeing more than they should. Melts into the ground and reappears behind you without a sound.

BLOOD WOLVES

Bigger than any wolf, necks uncomfortably long — their eyes don't just hunger, they understand and wait. They hunt in coordinated packs… and the humans here eat their flesh to stay alive.

THE PRIEST · THE ORGANIZER

The sixth: "neither highest nor lowest — he is the one who connects." Floats above the dome with his red staff, sweeps the sector harvesting humans into camps — and at eclipse lines them up around it as offerings.

AIGORTHAL · THE SO-CALLED GOD

The seventh, arriving with the eclipse. Neither being nor nothingness: a web of limbs belonging to people who never finished, its core pulsing red like a signal — "I am here… though I should not be." A bare skull for a face; two candles above him that give no light, only proving time rots here. His lower half is another creature entirely — an armored crawler, prisoner of its own body, and its body its prison. He devours the priest alive, then feeds on thousands.
